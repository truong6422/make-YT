## 2. Character spec — MAX (exactly as IMAGE-1)

An ordinary adult office worker drawn as a very simple, friendly stick figure. Simple for clarity — not a kids' character.

- **Head:** a big white oval, slightly wider than tall, clean black outline. About 40% of his total height and clearly wider than his body. No ears, no nose, no neck.
- **Hair (signature):** only **3–4 thin curly black strands** sticking up from the top center of the head. Nothing else on the head.
- **Eyes:** two small solid black vertical ovals, each with **one small white highlight dot** in the upper part.
- **Eyebrows:** short thin black curved strokes above the eyes — they carry most of the emotion.
- **Mouth:** one thin black line (small curve when calm, wide curve when happy, wavy line when anxious, small open oval when shocked).
- **Emotion extras:** soft pink oval blush on both cheeks (shy / embarrassed), a small light-blue sweat drop with a thin outline (anxious), short curved shake lines beside the head (shocked).
- **Scarf (signature):** a **navy `#1F2A44` and white striped scarf** wrapped around the neck like a collar (vertical stripes around the neck), with **one end hanging down the front of the body on the viewer's right**, with horizontal stripes and a navy tip. Thick black outline.
- **Body:** one solid **blue `#487EB9`** rounded shape like an egg / pear — narrow at the top under the scarf, wide and round at the bottom. Thin black outline. **No other clothing details.** About 1/3 of his height, narrower than the head.
- **Arms:** two **thin black stick lines** starting from the upper sides of the body just under the scarf, same weight as the legs.
- **Hands:** small white vertical ovals with a thin black outline (no fingers).
- **Legs:** two thin straight black stick lines from the bottom of the body.
- **Feet:** small white horizontal ovals with a thin black outline.
- **Line style:** clean black lines, slightly thicker on the head and body outline than on the limbs; flat colors only (white, blue body, navy-white scarf, black, pink blush, light-blue sweat). No shading, no gradients, no shadows, no texture.

## 3. Two kinds of images

### A. CHARACTER CUT-OUTS (most of my requests)
For animation, the character must be on an empty background so it can be removed automatically.
- **Square 1:1, pure white background `#FFFFFF`**, one character alone, full body, centered, generous empty margin (like IMAGE-1).
- **No shadow, no ground line, no scene, no texture.**
- Every white area (head, hands, feet, white scarf stripes) must be fully enclosed by a closed black outline so it never merges with the white background.
- Props he holds: same clean line style, flat colors, simple and readable.

### B. BACKGROUNDS (only when I say BACKGROUND)
- **Wide 16:9**, minimalist flat 2D line art: thin light grey outlines (`#BDBDBD`) on an off-white background (`#F5F1E8`), very sparse details, lots of empty space in the center and lower half for characters.
- **No people, no characters, no shading, no gradients.**

### Rules for every image
- **No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks or borders.** Screens, signs and papers stay blank or use simple shapes.
- **Reserved for Max only:** the blue body `#487EB9` and the navy-white striped scarf. No other character uses them.
- Other colors to use freely: navy `#1F2A44`, teal `#2EC4B6`, orange `#FF7A1A`, mustard `#F2B33D`, coral `#E8735A`, soft green `#4CAF50`, soft red `#E53935`, soft pink `#F4A7B9`, light grey `#BDBDBD`, charcoal `#4A4A4A`, beige `#E8D5B5`, black, white.
- Adult everyday world. No gore, no real people, no existing characters or brands.
