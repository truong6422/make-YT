## Mô tả

Max doesn't believe in fortune tellers. Then one sentence from a $20 street-fair reading — "Something bad is coming" — follows him for three months, and when his phone cracks, he's sure it came true.

In this animated story we look at why our brains fall for vague predictions: the Barnum effect, self-fulfilling prophecies, confirmation bias and our need for control — plus Skinner's "superstitious" pigeons, Malinowski's fishermen and Forer's famous fake personality test.

Educational animation about psychology. Not medical, mental-health or financial advice.
